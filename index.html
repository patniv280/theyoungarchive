who we are

A platform for students, not a portfolio for us.

The Young Archive is a creative & literary publication dedicated to showcasing and celebrating young talent. We provide a platform for students to share their work, gain recognition, and become part of a growing creative community.

"For things worth keeping, not just things worth posting."

what we keep

Three shelves, one growing archive.

✎ Writing

Poetry, prose, personal essays — anything that's been sitting half-finished in a drafts folder.

✿ Art

Illustration, painting, mixed media, mood boards — work that says something a sentence can't.

❀ Photography

Single frames or small series — the art of slowing down enough to notice something.
