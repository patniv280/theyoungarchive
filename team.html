the people ✎

Behind every issue.

Purvi and Vanshika founded The Young Archive — and a small, growing team helps read, design, and put every issue together.

Purvi Dhankani

Founder

"I'm Purvi, an aspiring designer and founder of The Young Archive, with a passion for visual storytelling, creative direction, and building ideas from scratch. I've explored design through internships and hands-on creative projects, while constantly experimenting with new ways to turn thoughts and emotions into visuals. I'm drawn to work that feels intentional, expressive, and a little different."

Vanshika Wadhwani

Founder

"I'm Vanshika, a writer, published poet, and founder of The Young Archive, with a strong interest in storytelling, branding, and creative marketing. I'm the author of my poetry anthology, If I Were The Moon, and my work has been published in freepress newspaper twice. I love combining words, ideas, and visuals to create work that feels personal yet meaningful."
