Who are we?

The short version: two students who got tired of good writing living and dying in a Notes app.

our mission

The Young Archive is a student-led creative publication dedicated to showcasing and celebrating young talent. We provide a platform for students to share their work, gain recognition, and become part of a growing creative community.

founded by

Purvi Dhankani
& Vanshika Wadhwani

The magazine started as a small idea between two students and a shared frustration: too much good writing was staying private. Three issues later, it's grown into a volunteer-run publication with its own submission cycles, mood boards, and a small but committed readership.

how it works ✿

From your drafts folder to the archive.

  1. 01

    An issue opens

    Every issue starts with a theme and a submission window announced on Instagram and here on the site.

  2. 02

    You submit

    Writing, art or photography, sent through our submission form before the deadline. Every submission gets read.

  3. 03

    We archive it

    Selected work is laid out, credited, and published as part of the issue — permanently on record, not scrolled past.
